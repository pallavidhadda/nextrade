# Competitive brief: AI stock research (draft, 2026-10-25)

Evidence quality: mostly vendor/affiliate comparison pages and secondary roundups; no product was used hands-on. Treat as leads to verify, not facts.

## Landscape
| Group | Examples | What they do well |
|---|---|---|
| Global AI research | Fiscal.ai (ex-FinChat), Perplexity Finance, AlphaSense, Koyfin | Plain-English fundamentals Q&A, answers cited to filings, transcripts search |
| Systematic scoring | Danelfin | Explainable 1-10 style scores, rule-based |
| India screeners | Screener.in, Tickertape, Trendlyne | Long fundamental history, custom screens/alerts, 1000+ parameters, DVM score (Trendlyne) |
| India AI add-ons | Sentiquant, Swastika Sarthi, MarketsMojo | Score + entry/stop/targets + thesis (claims unverified) |
| Open source agents | TradingAgents, ai-hedge-fund, FinRobot, OpenBB | Multi-agent debate, investor personas, report generation, data platform |
