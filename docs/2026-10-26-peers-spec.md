# Peer groups and relative valuation: spec (compact; builds on the earlier FinRobot study)
Research/education only; not advice.
Goal: for a target company, pick comparable companies deterministically from the loaded pool, compare matched multiples against the peer MEDIAN, and show the full trace of who was picked, who was excluded and why.
Design (my calls): Tier 1 = same Yahoo industry, Tier 2 = same sector. Eligibility: market cap known; size ceiling 20x the target for every tier; size floor 1/200 for Tier 1 and 1/20 for Tier 2 (FinRobot found a symmetric 1/20 floor excludes every real peer of a mega-cap leader); trailing P/E must be positive (loss-makers excluded). Ranking within a tier: |log(peer cap / target cap)| ascending, ties alphabetical; fill up to k=5, Tier 1 first. Metrics: P/E and P/B (matched equity multiples), ROE, net margin, YoY profit growth. Medians use only valid values: P/E in (0, 75], P/B in (0, 50]; a peer with an extreme P/E stays a member but is kept out of the median. Need >= 3 valid peer values, else unavailable with reason. Output: target value, peer median, premium/discount, position among peers, n.
Not in v1: EV/EBITDA (vendor EBITDA is unreliable), own-history percentile (no free historical multiples), sum-of-the-parts for conglomerates (flag only), Yahoo classification quality checks.
Tests PE1-PE14: determinism under shuffle; tier order beats size closeness; size ceiling and per-tier floors; non-positive or missing P/E excluded with reason; high P/E member but out of median; k limit with alphabetical ties; target never in own peers; fewer than 3 valid values unavailable; median not mean; premium math (20 vs 16 = +25%); missing market cap excluded; missing target cap or industry/sector unavailable; every pick carries tier, size ratio, reason and input fact ids; render has PEERS section, no buy/sell words.
